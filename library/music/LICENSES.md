# Music licences

Checked on 2026-11-17. Sources can change their terms: open the track's `source_url` before an important
release.

## CC0 1.0 — HoliznaCC0 (7 tracks)
Public-domain dedication. Use, edit and redistribute, including in paid advertising, with no credit.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Checked: each album page on Free Music Archive (`freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/<album>`) carried the
CC0 1.0 licence link when the files were downloaded from that page's own file links. Albums used:
`background-music`, `city-slacker`, `summer-air-lo-fi`, `public-domain-lofi`.

## CC BY 4.0 — Kevin MacLeod, incompetech.com (5 tracks)
Free for commercial use **if the music is credited**. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The composer's licence page (incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/) says of the free Creative Commons
option: "No charge. Requires that you credit the music." He also sells a no-credit licence per track, the
route to take if DOMUS wants one of these in a paid ad with no credit line.

Credit line, in the caption or on screen (also printed by `node library/bin/audio.mjs credits <id>`):

> "Title" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) — Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 —
> https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Files are the composer's own MP3s, unmodified, downloaded from `incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/mp3-royaltyfree/`.

| Scott Buckley (CC BY 4.0, cinematic) | Site refused automated requests; tracks could not be listed or verified from here. Worth adding by hand. |

| Free Music Archive in general | Licence varies per track, much of it non-commercial. Only the CC0 artist above was taken. |
