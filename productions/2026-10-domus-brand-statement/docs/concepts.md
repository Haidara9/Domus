# Concept: "The carousel, breathing" (direction 4: premium graphic motion, strong brand intensity)

The posts already define the system: paper, copper bracket, rule, line-art arches, counter. The film turns each
slide into a 4.6 s chapter in which the architecture **draws itself** while the statement settles. That way the brand
reads as built, careful and transparent, matching its values.

| 2 شريككم العقاري | 4.6-9.2 | new arch set draws from the left; same grammar | swell into the chapter; click on 02 |
| 3 ما نؤمن به | 9.2-13.8 | three arches, no column; values line | swell; click on 03 |
| 4 Brand | 13.8-18.0 | logo center-out reveal, rules extend, light sweep; services line; اللاذقية in copper | swell → soft low impact; shimmer with the sweep |
